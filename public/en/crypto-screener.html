What is h00k?

h00k is a free market information and analysis service for traders who want an overview of crypto markets without juggling many tabs. Guests can view the coin list and basic metrics. Certain charting, customization and alert features require an account.

Filter crypto pairs by 24-hour trading volume

Select the exchange and choose spot or futures. In the volume filter, use “from” to keep pairs at or above the selected threshold, or “to” for an upper limit. When searching for BTCUSDT, check the market shown in the row before opening its chart. Spot and futures have separate quotes and volume.

Frequently asked questions

Is h00k free?

Yes. Currently available site features are free, although some tools require registration.

Does h00k execute trades?

The screener offers information and practice tools. It does not place exchange orders on a user's behalf by itself.

Are quotes always real-time?

Prices depend on exchange feeds, connectivity and system availability. Delays are possible; always verify prices with the exchange before trading.
